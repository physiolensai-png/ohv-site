About

Software designed around the people who use it.

Olympus Hills Ventures is a software company built to create solutions for everyday problems. From personal health to professional productivity, we find better ways for people to have access to their own data and to be more efficient in how they use it.


We believe the best software disappears into the background — quietly doing the work so people can focus on what actually matters to them.

Everyday problems

We focus on friction people experience every day — in health, habits, work, and decisions.

Data that belongs to you

People should understand and own their own information. We build surfaces that make that real.

Built to last

Every product we ship is meant to be used, maintained, and improved — not abandoned.

Thesis Project #01

Enva: Unsiloying Personal Health Intelligence

A live case study in end-to-end execution—taking a complex personal friction point and building a full-stack platform to bring actionable health data to everyone.

Personal Health Intelligence Platform

Releasing Late '26

01. The Origin & Friction

I was taking 15+ screenshots of every workout, feeding them into LLMs, and running custom prompts to extract recovery trends. The insights were game-changing, but the manual investment was far too high. Enva was built to automate this friction and bring deep health analysis to everyone.

02. Functional vs. Practical UX

Functional: Consolidating fragmented data pipelines from wearables, nutrition, and workouts.
Practical: Designing the front-end interface around human context. Data without meaning is noise; practical UX explains why you should care.

03. AI-Native Build

Engineered entirely by treating LLMs as peer developers. Using AI agents to co-write, validate, and refactor features across the stack dramatically reduced time-to-market without sacrificing code architecture.
